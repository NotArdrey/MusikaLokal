# Booking and Payment Policies

## Booking Policy
All bookings made through the MusikaLokal platform differ in nature but follow a unified request-and-acceptance protocol to ensure security and mutual consent. When a Guest (Musician or Booker) submits a reservation request for a Studio or Gig, a binding offer is created which the Host (Venue or Studio Owner) may accept or decline within 24 hours. A booking is considered officially confirmed only upon the Host’s acceptance, at which point a contract is formed directly between the Host and the Guest. MusikaLokal acts solely as a facilitator for this transaction and is not a party to the rental or performance agreement. Guests are responsible for reviewing all listing details, house rules, and availability prior to booking. Failure to arrive within 30 minutes of the scheduled start time without prior notice may be classified as a "No-Show," resulting in the forfeiture of the booking session without eligibility for a refund, subject to the Host's discretion.

## Payment Policy
To ensure trust and safety, MusikaLokal employs a secure, escrow-based payment processing system. All financial transactions must be conducted exclusively through the MusikaLokal platform; any attempt to circumvent this system by arranging offline or direct payments is a violation of our Terms of Service and may result in immediate account suspension. Upon confirmation of a booking, the total estimated cost—including the Host’s rate and MusikaLokal’s service fees—is collected from the Guest’s payment method. These funds are held in a secure escrow account and are not released to the Host until 24 hours after the successful completion of the booking. This "hold period" allows both parties to report any issues or discrepancies. Payouts are made to the Host’s designated financial account (e.g., Bank Transfer, GCash, Maya) net of applicable service fees. In the event of a dispute, MusikaLokal reserves the right to freeze the disputed funds until a resolution is reached in accordance with our Dispute Resolution Guidelines.
